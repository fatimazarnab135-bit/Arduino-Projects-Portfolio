Soil Moisture Detection System:

Description:
This Arduino-based project monitors soil moisture levels and helps determine when plants need watering.

Features:
Soil moisture measurement
Real-time monitoring
Water level indication
Simple and cost-effective design

Components Used:
Arduino Uno
Soil Moisture Sensor
LEDs
Jumper Wires
Breadboard

Working Principle:
The soil moisture sensor measures the water content in the soil. Based on the moisture level, the system indicates whether the soil is wet, moist, or dry.

Applications:
Smart irrigation systems
Agriculture
Gardening
Plant monitoring
